This document outlines exactly what our AI research agent investigates when commissioned to assess escape room business opportunities in any given US state. Each report is generated fresh using live data, covers six core research areas, and is delivered as a professional HTML document ready for PDF conversion. Reports are addressed to Wendy and include an executive summary, a recommended locations table, and a risks section.

Research Areas Covered

  1. State Demographics & Market Profile — including inbound tourism and university populations
  2. Escape Room Market Landscape — competitor mapping, saturation, pricing
  3. Business Opportunity Assessment — recommended cities, greenfield markets, tourism & student hotspots
  4. Commercial Real Estate & Location Data — prime sites, lease rates, Wendy's site requirements
  5. Regulatory & Operational Environment — licences, safety codes, grants
  6. Financial Benchmarks — revenue, build costs, break-even
1
State Demographics & Market Profile
Establishes the fundamental market conditions — who lives there, who visits, and how strong the core escape room audience is before a single venue is assessed.

Core Demographics

  • Total state population and population density
  • Age distribution with specific focus on the 18–45 core escape room demographic
  • Household income levels and disposable income available for leisure spending
  • Urban vs rural split and the top metro areas by population
  • Education levels — used as a proxy for puzzle and experience appetite

Inbound Tourism & Visitor Economy KEY INDICATOR

  • Total annual visitor numbers to the state — domestic and international figures reported separately
  • Year-on-year tourism trend: growing, flat, or declining
  • Top 10 tourist attractions in the state ranked by annual visitor numbers — national monuments, parks, historical sites, theme parks, scenic landmarks
  • Which cities and regions capture the highest concentration of tourist traffic
  • Average visitor length of stay and daily spend on activities and entertainment
  • Peak tourist seasons and shoulder seasons mapped against escape room demand potential
  • Visitor demographic breakdown: family groups, couples, solo travellers, corporate and conference visitors
  • Convention and conference activity — major venues, annual events, trade shows (strong corporate group booking indicator)
  • State tourism board positioning data — where escape rooms could feature as a recommended activity
  • Rainy day and indoor activity demand — states with unpredictable weather see elevated demand for indoor entertainment from tourists
  • Cities identified where tourism is the primary economic driver — these represent captive audiences actively seeking activities

University & College Population KEY INDICATOR

  • Full list of all universities and colleges in the state with city location and total enrolled student population
  • Top 5 cities ranked by student population concentration
  • Cities where students exceed 10% of the local population are flagged as HIGH OPPORTUNITY markets
  • Campus proximity to city centres — walkable vs car-dependent
  • Student entertainment spending patterns
  • Academic calendar: peak demand periods (semester time) vs low periods (summer, holidays) — revenue planning implications
  • Presence of Greek life (fraternities and sororities) — strong group booking demand indicator
  • Postgraduate and international student population — higher disposable income segment
2
Escape Room Market Landscape
Maps the existing competitive environment across the state — who is already operating, how saturated the market is, and where the gaps are.

Operator Mapping

  • Estimated total number of escape room venues currently operating in the state
  • Split between independent operators and franchise locations
  • Major franchise brands already present: The Escape Game, Breakout, Escapology, Room Escape Adventures, and others
  • Geographic distribution of venues — which cities are well served and which are underserved

Market Saturation Analysis

  • Venues per capita compared to the national average
  • Identification of cities with clear supply gaps relative to population
  • Assessment of whether the state overall is under-, over-, or optimally served

Pricing & Product Intelligence

  • Average ticket price per person across the state
  • Price range from budget to premium operators
  • Popular room themes and formats performing well in the region
  • Any emerging experience formats (horror, VR-hybrid, competitive, immersive theatre crossover)
3
Business Opportunity Assessment
Translates the demographic and market data into specific, actionable city-level recommendations — with the rationale for each clearly stated.

City Recommendations

  • Top 3–5 recommended cities or metro areas with a specific written rationale for each selection
  • Greenfield opportunities — markets with little or no existing escape room competition
  • Estimated total addressable market size per recommended city

Tourist Hotspot Assessment

  • For each high-tourism city identified in Section 1 — is existing escape room provision adequate for the visitor volume, or is the market underserved?
  • Whether dominant visitor types (families, couples, groups) match escape room audience profiles
  • Proximity to major attractions — venues near top-10 draws benefit from activity overflow
  • Rainy day positioning viability for tourist-heavy locations
  • Hotel and accommodation density nearby — guests actively seek evening and daytime activities
  • Online activity platform performance: TripAdvisor Experiences, Viator, GetYourGuide — do escape rooms perform strongly in comparable tourist cities?

University City Assessment

  • For each flagged high-student city — does an escape room already exist within 1 mile of campus?
  • Walk-in trade potential from student foot traffic
  • Group booking demand from sports teams, clubs, societies, and university department events
  • Student discount pricing strategy viability
  • Freshers week and orientation periods as major annual revenue spikes
  • Venue proximity to student union buildings, bars, and late-night entertainment strips

Market Context

  • Franchise vs independent operator considerations specific to this state
  • Seasonal demand factors — tourism peaks, university calendar, local events
  • Competition from adjacent entertainment: VR arcades, axe throwing, mini golf, bowling, immersive dining

Commercial Real Estate & Location Data Priority Section
The most detailed section of every report. Identifies prime, commercially viable sites that meet Wendy's specific operational requirements — and filters out anything that does not.

Wendy's Hard Site Requirements — Non-Negotiable

  • Minimum 2,000 sq ft — anything below this threshold is excluded from the report entirely
  • Target range: 3,500–6,000 sq ft to comfortably accommodate 4–6 escape rooms plus all supporting spaces
  • 4–6 escape rooms — each room requires approximately 250–400 sq ft of dedicated play space
  • Supporting spaces required: reception and waiting area (400–600 sq ft), GM monitoring station, corridors, bathrooms, storage, staff areas
  • Prime location — high footfall, strong visibility, passing trade potential
  • Reasonable price per sq ft — the report will flag what constitutes value vs overpriced for each specific market

Per-City Lease Rate Intelligence

  • Current average commercial lease rate per sq ft — monthly and annual — broken down by Class A, B, and C space
  • Clear definition of what "reasonable" vs "premium/overpriced" means in that specific market
  • Current vacancy rates for suitable commercial space in target areas
  • Any specific available properties matching the size criteria sourced from LoopNet, CoStar, Crexi, or local commercial listings

Prime Location Factors

  • Specific named neighbourhoods, districts, and streets with high footfall and visibility
  • Proximity priorities: entertainment districts, restaurants and bars, cinemas, bowling alleys, shopping centres, transport hubs, hotels, tourist attractions
  • Ground floor vs upper floor analysis — ground floor preferred for walk-in trade; upper floor may offer significantly lower rates
  • Parking availability — surface lots, structured parking, or street parking (essential for group bookings)
  • Loading access for large props, set dressing, and equipment delivery

Physical Property Requirements

  • Ceiling height — minimum 9ft recommended for immersive room design
  • Column-free floor plans preferred to allow flexible room configuration
  • Power supply capacity for lighting, audio, and puzzle electronics across 4–6 simultaneous rooms

Lease Structure Intelligence

  • Typical lease lengths in this market — 3, 5, and 10 year term norms
  • Whether landlords are offering rent-free periods or fit-out contributions for quality tenants
  • Triple net (NNN) vs gross lease norms for the state
  • Tenant improvement allowances typical in this market
  • Enterprise zone incentives or small business grants available to offset real estate costs

Red Flags — The Agent Will Actively Flag These

  • Areas with declining footfall or visible retail blight
  • Locations where parking is severely limited or expensive
  • Markets where commercial rents have spiked unsustainably in the past two years
  • Sites below the 2,000 sq ft minimum — these are excluded, not flagged

Regulatory & Operational Environment
Covers the legal and compliance landscape so there are no surprises after a site is selected.
  • State business registration requirements and process
  • Fire safety codes and occupancy limits applicable to immersive entertainment venues
  • Liquor licence availability and rules — relevant if a bar or drinks element is planned
  • State-level small business support programmes and incentive schemes
  • Any state-specific regulations relevant to escape rooms or immersive entertainment
6
Financial Benchmarks
Provides the financial context needed to evaluate viability and build a business case.
  • Industry average revenue per escape room per year
  • Typical venue fit-out and build cost range for this specific market
  • Estimated break-even timeline based on market conditions
  • Notable recent escape room openings, closures, or acquisitions in the state — market momentum indicators
