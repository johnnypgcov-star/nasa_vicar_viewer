Operator Context — Built Into Every Report

Every report generated by this research agent is tailored to Wendy's specific situation as an experienced operator. Wendy currently runs a 3-room escape room venue in a mall in Vancouver, Washington, established in 2022 and performing well. She is researching expansion into a new US state with a larger venue of 4–6 rooms at a minimum of 2,000 sq ft. Her proven model is mall-based, so the agent specifically prioritises mall and high-footfall retail entertainment centre opportunities in every state assessed. All city and site recommendations are benchmarked against her existing Vancouver, WA market so she has a direct point of comparison.

This document outlines exactly what the research agent investigates for each state report. Reports are generated fresh using live data, cover six research areas in depth, and are delivered as professional HTML documents ready for PDF conversion.

Research Areas Covered

  1. State Demographics & Market Profile — economic health, net migration & tax burden, inbound tourism, university populations, family & school holiday market
  2. Escape Room Market Landscape — competitor mapping, future competitive pipeline, search demand, discount health check, customer review sentiment
  3. Business Opportunity Assessment — city recommendations, Vancouver WA benchmark, corporate market, events, tourism, student & family hotspots, franchise options
  4. Commercial Real Estate & Location Data — mall opportunities, crime & safety data, prime sites, lease rates, Wendy's site requirements
  5. Regulatory & Operational Environment — licences, safety codes, ADA, permitting timelines, tax incentives & business investment credits
  6. Financial Benchmarks & Operating Costs — revenue, build costs, staffing, insurance, technology, ancillary revenue, digital marketing & customer acquisition costs
1
State Demographics & Market Profile
Establishes fundamental market conditions — who lives there, who visits, whether the population is growing or shrinking, and the economic confidence of the consumer base.

Core Demographics

  • Total population and density
  • Age distribution with focus on the 18–45 core escape room demographic
  • Household income and disposable income available for leisure spending
  • Urban vs rural split and top metro areas
  • Education levels — proxy for puzzle and experience appetite

Economic Health & Employment

  • Current unemployment rate for the state and each recommended city
  • Job growth trend over the past 3 years — expanding or contracting economy?
  • Major employers and dominant industries (diverse economies are more resilient)
  • Consumer confidence and discretionary spending trends
  • Any significant recent employer arrivals or departures that have shifted the landscape

Net Migration, Population Movement & Tax Environment CRITICAL VIABILITY CHECK

  • Net domestic migration — people arriving from other US states vs leaving (IRS migration data and US Census Bureau)
  • Net international migration
  • 5-year population growth or decline trend
  • State income tax rate — flagged if among the highest nationally; noted if no income tax (strong pull factor)
  • State sales tax and property tax rates compared nationally
  • Overall state tax burden using the Tax Foundation State Business Tax Climate Index
  • Recent tax increases that coincided with population exodus
  • Business tax environment — corporation tax, major employer relocations out of state
  • City-level population trends within the state — growing vs declining cities identified
  • Where incomers are arriving from, and where residents are leaving to
  • Cost of living index for the state and key cities
  • States with consistent net outmigration and a high or rising tax burden are flagged HIGH RISK in the Executive Summary

Inbound Tourism & Visitor Economy KEY INDICATOR

  • Total annual visitor numbers — domestic and international separately
  • Year-on-year tourism trend: growing, flat, or declining
  • Top 10 tourist attractions ranked by annual visitor count — national monuments, parks, historical sites, theme parks, landmarks
  • Cities and regions with the highest tourist traffic concentration
  • Average visitor length of stay and daily activity spend
  • Peak and shoulder seasons mapped to escape room demand
  • Visitor demographics: families, couples, solo travellers, corporate and conference visitors
  • Convention and conference activity — trade shows and major events bring large groups (strong corporate booking signal)
  • Rainy day and indoor activity demand — unpredictable weather states see elevated indoor entertainment demand
  • Cities where tourism is the primary economic driver — captive audiences seeking activities

University & College Population KEY INDICATOR

  • All universities and colleges listed with city location and total enrolled students
  • Top 5 cities by student population concentration
  • Cities where students exceed 10% of local population flagged as HIGH OPPORTUNITY markets
  • Campus proximity to city centres — walkable vs car-dependent
  • Student entertainment spending patterns
  • Academic calendar: peak demand (semester time) vs low periods (summer, holidays)
  • Greek life presence (fraternities and sororities) — strong group booking demand indicator
  • Postgraduate and international students — higher disposable income segment

Family & School Holiday Market

  • Proportion of the population that are families with children aged 8–16 — the core family escape room demographic
  • Number of primary and secondary schools in recommended cities
  • State school holiday calendar — specific weeks driving peak family bookings (spring break, summer, Thanksgiving, Christmas, winter break)
  • Whether recommended cities are family-oriented leisure destinations or primarily student, tourist, or corporate markets
  • Presence of family entertainment competitors (trampoline parks, bowling, laser tag) — confirms proven family leisure spend in the area
2
Escape Room Market Landscape
Maps the existing competitive environment — who is operating, how healthy those operators are, and where the genuine gaps in the market are.

Market Overview

  • Total number of escape room venues operating in the state
  • Split between independent operators and franchise locations
  • Major franchise brands present: The Escape Game, Breakout, Escapology, Room Escape Adventures, and others
  • Saturation analysis: venues per capita vs national average
  • Geographic distribution — which cities are well served and which are underserved
  • Average ticket price per person, group package rates, and corporate rates
  • Popular themes, formats, and any emerging experience types in the region

Google Search Demand

  • Monthly search volume for "[city] escape room" for each recommended city — a real-time proxy for consumer demand
  • High search volume relative to few existing venues signals a clear supply gap
  • Seasonality in search trends across the state

Future Competitive Pipeline

  • Announced escape room openings, new venue launches, or franchise expansion announcements for the state and recommended cities
  • Franchise brand expansion press releases — national chains often announce territory openings months in advance
  • Local business news and commercial real estate publications searched for pipeline activity
  • A confirmed major competitor opening within 12 months is flagged prominently — it materially changes the entry calculus
  • A city with no pipeline activity and current underservice is a stronger opportunity signal

Discount Platform Health Check

  • Whether existing operators are actively discounting on Groupon, LivingSocial, or similar platforms
  • Heavy sustained discounting is a red flag — typically indicates venues unable to fill rooms at full price
  • Operators relying on Groupon as their primary marketing channel are flagged as financially stressed

Competitor Sentiment Analysis — Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Facebook, Instagram

  • Star rating recorded for every named venue across all available platforms, alongside total review volume
  • Most common praise themes in positive reviews: immersive design, staff, puzzle quality, value, atmosphere, production value
  • Most common complaint themes in negative reviews: outdated rooms, poor tech, unhelpful staff, overpriced, too short, maintenance issues
  • Instagram and Facebook visual quality assessment from customer-shared photos
  • Venues with unresolved complaints or declining review trends flagged
  • Venues where customers explicitly say they would not return or recommend flagged separately
  • Recurring unmet customer desires identified — direct product opportunities for a new entrant
  • Ancillary revenue streams visible from each operator's website and social presence
Market Opportunity Signal

Venue rated below 3.8 stars or consistent negative sentiment → city flagged as MARKET OPPORTUNITY — demand present, supply quality poor

Competitive Threat Signal

Venue rated above 4.5 stars with high review volume → flagged as ESTABLISHED COMPETITOR — differentiated product required

Business Opportunity Assessment
Translates all demographic, tourism, student, economic, and competitor data into specific, ranked city recommendations with clear rationale for each.

City Recommendations

  • Top 3–5 recommended cities with written rationale for each selection
  • Greenfield opportunities — markets with little or no existing competition
  • Estimated total addressable market size per city
  • Review-informed opportunity scoring — cities where existing venues have poor reviews and high demand rank highest

Vancouver, WA Benchmark Comparison

  • For each recommended city, key indicators compared directly to Wendy's existing Vancouver, WA market: population, household income, escape room density, average ticket price, and commercial lease rates
  • Gives Wendy a clear "how does this compare to what I already know" reference for every recommendation

Corporate & Team-Building Market MAJOR REVENUE STREAM

  • Density of corporate headquarters, large office employers, and business parks in each recommended city
  • Dominant industries assessed for team-building budget levels (tech, finance, healthcare, and legal firms tend to spend most)
  • Whether existing operators are actively targeting corporate clients (checked via their websites)
  • Proximity of recommended site locations to major business districts and office parks
  • Conference hotel presence nearby — conference delegates are prime corporate booking targets

Major Local Events & Anchor Calendar

  • Annual events creating predictable booking spikes: sports championships, Comic-Con conventions, music festivals, state fairs
  • Professional sports team presence (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL) and game-day foot traffic patterns
  • Major recurring conventions or trade shows
  • Holiday and seasonal demand peaks beyond summer and Christmas

Tourist Hotspot Assessment

  • Escape room provision relative to visitor volume — underserved relative to footfall?
  • Dominant visitor types aligned with escape room demographics
  • Proximity to major attractions — venues near top-10 draws benefit from activity overflow
  • Rainy day indoor positioning viability, hotel density nearby
  • Online activity platform performance on TripAdvisor Experiences, Viator, GetYourGuide

University City Assessment

  • Does an escape room already exist within 1 mile of campus?
  • Walk-in trade and group booking demand from student clubs, sports teams, and university departments
  • Freshers week and orientation periods as major annual revenue spikes
  • Proximity to student bars and late-night entertainment strips

Franchise Opportunities

  • Whether any major national escape room franchise brands are actively seeking franchisees in this state
  • If so: franchise fee, royalty structure, territory terms, and support offered
  • Assessment of whether franchise or independent operation is more appropriate given Wendy's existing experience

Multi-Site & Operational Scaling Considerations

  • Wendy is expanding from 1 to 2 venues — factors specific to running multiple escape room locations noted (staffing, booking systems, brand consistency)
  • Local availability of experienced escape room or entertainment venue staff
  • Weekend vs weekday demand balance — purely weekend markets have lower overall utilisation potential

Commercial Real Estate & Location Data Priority Section
The most detailed section of every report. Identifies prime, commercially viable sites meeting Wendy's specific requirements — and filters out anything that falls short.

Wendy's Hard Site Requirements — Non-Negotiable

  • Minimum 2,000 sq ft — anything below is excluded from the report entirely
  • Target range: 3,500–6,000 sq ft for 4–6 rooms plus all supporting spaces
  • 4–6 escape rooms — each room ~250–400 sq ft playable space
  • Supporting spaces: reception/waiting (400–600 sq ft), GM station, corridors, bathrooms, storage, staff areas
  • Prime location — high footfall, visibility, passing trade
  • Reasonable price per sq ft — the report defines value vs overpriced for each specific market

Mall & Retail Entertainment Centre Opportunities WENDY'S PROVEN MODEL

  • Malls and retail entertainment centres in each recommended city specifically identified and assessed for available space meeting the size criteria
  • Each mall graded: A-grade (premium, high footfall, national anchors) / B-grade (solid community mall, good footfall) / C-grade (declining — avoid)
  • Co-tenancy assessment: malls with cinema, bowling, arcade, food court create cross-pollination of customers — strong location signal
  • Whether the mall has a dedicated entertainment zone or is repositioning toward experience retail (post-pandemic trend)
  • Mall-specific lease terms: longer leases often offset by generous fit-out contributions and rent-free periods for entertainment tenants who drive footfall
  • Inline vs end-cap vs anchor position within the mall — visibility and passing trade implications
  • Parking — malls typically solve the group booking parking problem

Per-City Lease Rate Intelligence

  • Average commercial lease rate per sq ft (monthly and annual) broken down by Class A, B, and C space
  • Clear definition of what "reasonable" vs "overpriced" means for that specific market
  • Current vacancy rates for suitable commercial space
  • Specific available properties sourced from LoopNet, CoStar, Crexi, or local listings where possible

Prime Location Factors (Beyond Malls)

  • Named neighbourhoods, districts, and streets with high footfall — entertainment districts, high streets, waterfront areas
  • Proximity to restaurants, bars, cinemas, bowling, transport hubs, hotels, tourist attractions
  • Ground floor vs upper floor trade-off — ground floor preferred for walk-in trade; upper floor may offer lower rates
  • Loading access for large props and equipment delivery

Physical Property Requirements

  • Ceiling height minimum 9ft for immersive room design
  • Column-free floor plans preferred for flexible room configuration
  • Power supply capacity for lighting, audio, and puzzle electronics across 4–6 simultaneous rooms

Lease Structure Intelligence

  • Typical lease lengths — 3, 5, 10 year term norms in this market
  • Rent-free periods and fit-out contributions available from landlords
  • Triple net (NNN) vs gross lease norms for the state
  • Tenant improvement allowances
  • Enterprise zone incentives or small business grants to offset real estate costs

Crime & Safety Data — Per Recommended Neighbourhood

  • Neighbourhood-level crime statistics for every recommended area (FBI data, local police statistics, NeighborhoodScout, AreaVibes)
  • Violent crime rate and property crime rate assessed relative to city and national averages
  • Above-average crime areas are flagged — customers, particularly families and women's groups, avoid areas they perceive as unsafe after dark
  • Staff safety considerations for evening and late-night shifts
  • High-crime areas attract higher commercial insurance premiums — the connection is noted in the financial section
  • Low-crime, well-lit, busy locations flagged positively — safety perception drives customer confidence and repeat bookings

Red Flags — Actively Flagged in the Report

  • C-grade or dying malls — cheap rent does not compensate for low footfall
  • Areas with retail blight or high nearby vacancy rates
  • Severely limited or expensive parking
  • Markets where commercial rents have spiked unsustainably in the past 2 years
  • Cities in high-outmigration states where the customer base is shrinking
  • Neighbourhoods with above-average crime rates — particularly relevant for an evening entertainment venue

Regulatory & Operational Environment
Covers the legal and compliance landscape so there are no surprises after a site is selected.
  • State business registration requirements and typical timeline
  • Fire safety regulations, occupancy limits, and emergency exit requirements for immersive and low-light venues
  • ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) compliance — state-specific requirements that may exceed federal standards; fit-out cost implications noted
  • Liquor licence availability and rules — relevant if a bar or drinks service is planned
  • Permitting & Build Timeline: realistic time from lease signing to doors open in this state, including building permits, fire marshal inspections, and occupancy certificates; known bottlenecks flagged
  • Contractor availability for specialist fit-out work in recommended cities
  • State small business support programmes, grants, and mentorship schemes
  • Any state-specific regulations relevant to escape rooms or immersive entertainment venues

Tax Incentives & Business Investment Credits POSITIVE OPPORTUNITY MODIFIER

Genuine tax incentives can materially improve the financial case for an already attractive state. They are treated as a positive modifier — never as a reason to enter a state that scores poorly on migration, economic health, or market fundamentals.

  • Federal Opportunity Zones — whether recommended cities or neighbourhoods fall within a designated Opportunity Zone (IRS and CDFI Fund maps); offers capital gains tax deferral and potential reduction for investments held 5–10 years
  • State Enterprise Zones / Economic Development Zones — property tax abatements, local income tax credits, reduced business rates, or sales tax exemptions for businesses setting up and creating jobs in targeted areas
  • New Markets Tax Credit (NMTC) — federal programme offering tax credits for investments in low-income communities; eligibility checked for recommended cities
  • State Small Business Tax Credits — credits for job creation, capital investment, or locating in underserved areas; sourced from state department of revenue or NCSL database
  • State Small Business Credit Initiative (SSBCI) — federally funded, state-administered capital support for small businesses
  • Sales tax exemptions on fit-out materials and business equipment — directly reduces build costs
  • Local city or county incentives — rate relief, BID support, marketing grants, reduced permit fees for new businesses in regeneration areas
  • Entertainment or tourism-specific incentives — any state programmes designed to attract tourism or entertainment businesses
  • First-year or start-up tax relief — reduced or waived business taxes in the first 1–3 years of operation
  • SBA loan programmes — active Small Business Administration lending or preferred lenders in the state for fit-out financing
For Each Incentive Found, the Report States:
✓  What the incentive is and who administers it
✓  Whether recommended cities and sites qualify
✓  Estimated financial value to Wendy's specific scenario
✓  Any conditions, minimum investment thresholds, or job creation requirements
✓  Whether the incentive is time-limited or subject to renewal

Where meaningful incentives are found, they are flagged in the Executive Summary and reflected positively in the State Scorecard financial viability score. Where none exist, this is stated clearly.

Financial Benchmarks & Operating Costs
Provides the full financial picture needed to assess viability — from build costs and revenue benchmarks through to staffing, insurance, and ancillary income potential.

Revenue & Build Cost Benchmarks

  • Industry average revenue per escape room per year (national benchmark)
  • Estimated revenue potential for a 4–6 room venue in this specific market based on local pricing and realistic occupancy
  • Typical fit-out and build cost range — distinguished between basic and premium production quality
  • Estimated break-even timeline based on market conditions
  • Notable recent openings, closures, or acquisitions in the state — market momentum indicators

Labour Market & Staffing Costs

  • State minimum wage and any higher city-level minimum wages in recommended cities
  • Average hourly wages for hospitality and entertainment workers
  • Labour market tightness — easy or hard to hire in this market?
  • Estimated annual payroll for a typical 4–6 room venue staffing model at local wage rates
  • Whether existing operators face staffing challenges (evidenced by job postings or review mentions)

Insurance

  • Estimated commercial liability insurance costs for an immersive entertainment venue in this state
  • Whether the state has notably higher or lower insurance rates than the national norm
  • Specialist escape room or entertainment venue insurance providers operating in this market

Technology & Booking Systems

  • Leading booking and management platforms used by top-performing US escape room operators (Resova, Checkfront, Xola, Fareharbor, Escape Room Master, etc.)
  • Online gift card capability — a standard customer expectation; Christmas and birthday gift card revenue can be significant
  • Any dominant platform preferences or norms in this specific market

Ancillary Revenue Potential

  • Realistic estimate of ancillary revenue from corporate packages, private events, merchandise, and photo packages in this market
  • Gift card market assessment — how active is the experience gifting culture in the region?
  • What additional revenue streams are the most successful operators in the state already using — and what gaps remain?

Digital Marketing & Customer Acquisition Costs

  • Estimated Google Ads cost-per-click for escape room keywords in each recommended city — indicates advertising competition and cost to acquire customers
  • Whether existing operators are running paid search or social media ads — indicates market maturity
  • Active local Facebook community groups, Nextdoor, and city tourism websites available for low-cost marketing
  • Local influencers, bloggers, or YouTube channels that review escape rooms or local entertainment
  • Whether TripAdvisor, Yelp, or Google Business listings dominate local discovery — indicates where to prioritise review generation
  • Estimated customer acquisition cost range for this market — higher CAC in competitive markets directly affects the financial model
